About Rast Film

Where ancient wisdom meets the silver screen

Rast Film is an independent studio dedicated to bringing the timeless stories of ancient Persian civilization to life through cinema. Our name, Rast, means "truth" and "justice" in Persian — values at the heart of every story we tell.

From the Zoroastrian fire temples to the legendary workshops of Jamshid, from the epic battles of Rostam to the quiet wisdom of the Avesta — we craft films that bridge millennia, inviting audiences worldwide to experience the depth and beauty of a civilization that shaped human history.

Every frame is a dialogue between past and present. Every story is a bridge across cultures.
